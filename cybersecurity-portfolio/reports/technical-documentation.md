## Report Quality Checklist

Before publishing a report, confirm that it:

- Clearly states the objective and authorized scope.
- Distinguishes observed evidence from assumptions.
- Explains the tools and method used.
- Describes findings accurately.
- Includes risk reasoning and practical recommendations.
- Identifies what was and was not validated.
- Avoids credentials, secrets, personal data, and employer-confidential information.
- Uses sanitized screenshots and accurate project status.
