## Reporting Checklist

Before publishing or updating a case study, confirm that it:

- States the objective and authorized scope where relevant.
- Names only tools and configurations that are known to have been used.
- Distinguishes completed work from planned activities.
- Separates observed results from assumptions and general technical context.
- Includes evidence for specific findings, counts, identifiers, and conclusions.
- Explains limitations and what was not verified.
- Provides risk analysis and recommendations only when justified by the evidence.
- Identifies whether remediation was merely recommended, performed, or validated.
- Avoids credentials, secrets, personal data, sensitive target details, and employer-confidential information.
- Uses sanitized screenshots and accurate project status.
- Links to relevant project pages and trustworthy technical references where appropriate.
