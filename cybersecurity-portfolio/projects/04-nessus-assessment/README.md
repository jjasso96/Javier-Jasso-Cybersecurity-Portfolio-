## Vulnerability Management Context

A scan and its results review are parts of a broader vulnerability management process. A complete process may also include:

1. Confirming the authorized scope and identifying assets.
2. Running an appropriately configured scan.
3. Reviewing and validating scanner-reported findings.
4. Prioritizing verified issues based on severity, exploitability, exposure, and business impact.
5. Planning and implementing remediation.
6. Validating corrective actions, where applicable.
7. Reporting findings, evidence, limitations, and outstanding risk.

This lifecycle is included as professional context; it is not a claim that every step was completed in this exercise.

## Security Considerations

- Scan only systems explicitly authorized for testing.
- Follow the training platform's rules and the target's permitted scope.
- Treat automated scanner output as evidence to review, not automatic proof of exploitability.
- Remove credentials, tokens, sensitive host details, and other private information before publishing evidence.
- Do not claim remediation or risk reduction without validation evidence.
